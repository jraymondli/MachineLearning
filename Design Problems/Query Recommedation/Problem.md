Build a **query suggestion** module for the enterprise search product: surface candidate search queries when the user **has not typed anything yet** (e.g., pills below the search bar or a dropdown on focus). **This is *not* typeahead/autocomplete** — there is no prefix to complete; it’s a **zero-query recommendation** problem where the “items” being recommended are **queries**.

**What it establishes:** (a) the candidate can design an end-to-end system and reason about the product, and (b) **deep expertise in personalization algorithms**. **Algorithmic depth is the primary differentiator** — end-to-end system design is a competence *floor*; the L5/L6 signal is depth on candidate generation, **query clustering/canonicalization**, ranking, and **next-query sequence modeling**. The clearest **L6 signals** come from surfacing sophisticated understanding **unprompted** — a deep grasp of **query clustering** (why it matters simultaneously for signal quality, model features, and display), **trust & safety** as a first-class concern, and where **LLMs** genuinely help (grounded generation). No single one of these is *the* gate; raising any of them without being led is a staff signal.
